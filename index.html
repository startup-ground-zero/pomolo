Since 2022 at Mykonos

An Island Standard of Interior Craftsmanship

Pomolo was born on the island of Mykonos, where Cycladic light meets uncompromising design. We source and curate the finest bathroom fittings, furniture, textiles and outdoor living pieces for discerning homes, villas and hospitality projects across the Aegean and beyond.

Every piece in our showroom is chosen for one reason: it must embody our promise — Interior, Luxury, Quality.

Meet the Owner

Pomolo is shaped by a passion for Cycladic architecture and refined living. That point of view guides every piece selected for the showroom and every project created for Mykonos homes and villas.
